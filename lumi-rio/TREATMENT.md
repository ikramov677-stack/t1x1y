# «Город огня и город дождя» — cinematic 3D · treatment

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, Russian voice (ElevenLabs, later) + subtitles + `.srt`. ~49 s before the voice.
Characters: **Lumi** — small girl of glowing coal and embers, candle flame on her head, green knitted scarf; **Rio** — tall raindrop boy, clear blue water body, yellow raincoat.
Look: glowing characters on dark sets, soft cinematic light, warm orange vs cool blue, depth of field + bloom.
