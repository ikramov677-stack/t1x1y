# «Человек светится» — cinematic 3D · treatment

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, three.js rendered headless with the Lemo-Opuscar 3D post (physical depth of field, bloom, vignette/grade). Russian voice (ElevenLabs, later) + subtitles + `.srt`. ~75 s.
The user's prompts describe photoreal cinematic stills; with no image generator here, the user chose a stylised cinematic 3D version: dark sets, glow, shallow focus, film grain — people are sculpted mannequin figures, not photographs.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Lecture**: the experiment first, then the facts. Logical but slow to hook.
2. **You → proof → the world → you again (chosen)**: open on "you" glowing in the dark; the 2009 experiment proves it; the face map and the daily rhythm; the reason (cells); the wider glowing world (fireflies, deep sea, mushrooms); back to you switching off the light and lying in bed, still glowing; a starry town.
3. **A countdown from bright to dim** — every scene dimmer than the last. Elegant but hides the brightest image (the deep sea) in the middle.

## Look
Near-black sets lit by one cool key and a rim; the body's glow is a thin additive fresnel aura that bloom spreads; colour models per scene: cool blue room, lab, warm morning/evening window light with beams, magenta cells, green fireflies and mushrooms, cyan/magenta deep sea, starry night. Titles in Oswald with wide tracking and a soft glow; subtitles in Inter.

## Sound
Ambient score in D minor / F major: drones, felt piano, a slow pulse entering after the hook, shimmer pads in the second half, swelling to the end. Foley: a deep boom on the hook and the ending, camera shutters, a rising tone for the photo, clock ticks, birds at dawn, cell sparkles, crickets, underwater bed with bubbles, forest wind, the light switch click.

## Facts used
Ultra-weak photon emission imaged in 2009 by Japanese researchers (Kobayashi, Kikuchi, Okamura, Tohoku Institute of Technology / Kyoto University, PLoS ONE 2009): ~1000× below the eye's sensitivity; strongest from the face (cheeks, forehead, neck); lowest around 10:00, highest around 16:00; linked to metabolic free-radical reactions. Fireflies, deep-sea bioluminescence (most deep-sea animals), bioluminescent fungi.
